# Made hands and drawing hands

A draw can become powerful, but it is not a made hand yet.
{: .ff-page-intro }

## Tonight’s goal

On the flop, players should say what they currently have before describing what they might make.

> **First name your made hand. Then name your draw.**

## The ten-minute lesson

### 1 minute — ask

Show A♥ 7♥ on K♥ 9♥ 2♣. Ask: “Do we have a flush?”

No—we have ace high and a **flush draw**.

### 3 minutes — explain

| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Made hand | Has value right now | Pair, two pair, straight, flush |
| Drawing hand | Needs another card to complete | Flush draw, straight draw |
| Combination | A made hand plus a draw | Pair and flush draw |

### 3 minutes — deal examples

1. Q♠ J♠ on Q♦ 8♠ 3♠ — pair of queens **and** a flush draw.
2. 9♣ 8♦ on 7♥ 6♠ K♣ — open-ended straight draw, but only nine high now.
3. A♣ K♦ on A♦ 10♣ 4♠ — top pair, no immediate flush draw.

Ask players to name “now” before “later.”

### 2 minutes — avoid the common trap

A draw may miss. Do not call every bet just because completing the hand would be exciting.

Before continuing, ask:

- How many cards improve me?
- How much must I call?
- Could improving still leave me second best?

### 1 minute — set the challenge

> **Tonight’s mission:** Whenever you have a draw, say privately: “Right now I have…, and I am drawing to…”

Award the **Clear Thinker** recognition for accurately separating current strength from future potential.

## During the game

Ask:

- “What is your five-card hand right now?”
- “What card are you hoping to see?”
- “Is that card guaranteed to make you best?”

{: .table_tip }
Four cards of one suit are not a flush. Four connected cards are not a straight. Celebrate players who describe their hand accurately.

## Hand of the night

Choose one missed draw and one completed draw. Did the decision before the final card make sense regardless of the result?
