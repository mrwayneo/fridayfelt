# Bet for value

When you think you have the best hand, do not always check and hope.
{: .ff-page-intro }

## Tonight’s goal

Before betting, players should ask:

> **Can a worse hand call me?**

If the answer is yes, the bet may be a **value bet**.

## The ten-minute lesson

### 1 minute — ask

Ask: “If you believe you are winning, why might checking be a missed opportunity?”

### 3 minutes — explain

A value bet is not a guarantee that your hand is best. It is a bet made because enough worse hands may call.

| Reason to bet | Example |
|:--|:--|
| Get called by worse | Top pair may be called by a weaker pair |
| Build the pot | Strong hands earn more when chips go in |
| Charge draws | A player must pay to try to improve |

### 3 minutes — demonstrate

Board: K♣ 8♦ 3♠. Hero holds K♥ Q♥.

Ask which worse hands might call: K-J, K-10, an eight, or a straight draw such as 10-9.

Now change the board to A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♦ 10♣. Hero still has K-Q. Ask whether worse hands are likely to call a large bet. The board can change a value decision completely.

### 2 minutes — choose a friendly size

For tonight, use:

- **About half the pot** when unsure of sizing.
- Smaller when trying to be called by many weaker hands.
- Larger when many draws are available or worse strong hands can call.

The exact number matters less than knowing why the bet is being made.

### 1 minute — set the challenge

> **Tonight’s mission:** Before betting a made hand, name one worse hand that might call.

Award the **Value Hunter** recognition for a thoughtful bet that earns a call.

## During the game

Ask:

- “What worse hand might call?”
- “Are you betting because you are happy to be called?”
- “Did the new card make your hand stronger or the board more dangerous?”

{: .table_tip }
Do not judge a value bet as bad merely because a stronger hand calls. Judge whether enough worse hands could reasonably have called too.

## Hand of the night

Find one river decision where checking or betting were both possible. List the worse hands that might call, then list the better hands that would continue.
