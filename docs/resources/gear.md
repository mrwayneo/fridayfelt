# Poker gear without the guesswork

You only need chips, cards and a dealer button to run a great poker night. Start simple, buy durable gear once, and add the nice-to-haves only after your game becomes a regular tradition.
{: .ff-page-intro }

{: .table_tip }
For a family tournament of up to eight players, **500 chips is the comfortable choice**. A 300-chip set can work for smaller games, but check its denomination mix against your starting stacks before buying.

## The easiest complete setup

If you want one purchase that is ready for poker night, choose a **500-chip numbered set with a case**, add Copag cards and use any clear dealer button. A mat is the first worthwhile upgrade if you play on a hard dining table.

## Cards

### Just get Copag

A two-deck set of **Copag 100% plastic cards** is the straightforward recommendation. They are washable, durable and designed for repeated poker use. Bridge-size cards are slightly easier to deal for long sessions; poker-size cards feel more familiar if that is what your family already uses.

[Browse Copag playing cards](https://www.copagusa.com/){: .btn .btn-primary }

You do not need a different “premium” deck every season. Keep one deck in play and the second ready as a replacement.

### Dealer button

Buy one clear, easy-to-see dealer button. It does not need to be expensive; its job is simply to make position obvious on every hand.

### Cut card

Use a plastic cut card in a colour that contrasts with the deck. It prevents the bottom card being exposed and gives the person cutting the deck a clean target.

## Optional extras

- **All-in button:** fun and useful when chips have already been pushed forward, but not necessary.
- **Small and big blind buttons:** helpful for brand-new players, though the dealer button and table positions are enough once everyone understands the blinds.
- **Tournament timer:** use a phone or tablet before buying another physical device.
- **Chip racks:** useful for storage and colour-ups if you eventually outgrow a fitted case.

## Friday Felt starter checklist

- 500 numbered chips for up to eight or nine players
- Two Copag decks
- Dealer button
- Cut card
- Poker mat if the budget allows
- A simple tournament timer

That is enough. Good structure and a welcoming table will improve poker night far more than another box of accessories.
