# Every great poker story starts at home

Friday Felt began around a family poker table—with lessons before the game, a leaderboard on the wall, and a card protector passed from one winner to the next.
{: .ff-page-intro }

It exists to help friends and families create the same kind of tradition: welcoming enough for a first-time player, competitive enough to stay exciting, and memorable enough to continue for years.
